EcoVista Coffee Table

Modern lifestyles are increasingly driving people away from nature. This ecological landscape coffee table bridges that gap by integrating furniture with natural elements — a tempered glass tabletop reveals a miniature terrain of moss and wood beneath, promoting a sense of connection and tranquility in everyday living spaces.

02

EcoVista — Material Study

A tempered glass tabletop, a bed of live moss, and a solid wooden frame — three layers assembled to echo the earth's own texture. The design process moved from moodboard to material research to a full construction breakdown, encasing nature within a sleek, modern silhouette.

Material ResearchSustainabilityFurniture
03

Eco Ray Solar Lamp

Designed for remote areas in Hebei with unstable electricity, the Eco Ray Desk Lamp offers a stable, economical, and environmentally friendly solar lighting solution. Powered by solar energy and built with local visual language in mind, it's particularly suited to low-income and elderly households, ensuring light is available even during power outages.

03 — How I Work

The way I
approach a project.

01 / Understand
Ask before I sketch

Before opening any design tool, I take time to understand the goal, the audience, and the constraints — that shapes every decision that follows.

02 / Structure
Build the skeleton first

Good design starts with clear structure. I test flows and layouts at low fidelity before moving into visuals, to avoid rework later.

03 / Refine
Sweat the small things

Spacing, motion, materials, edge cases — these easily-overlooked details are where craft actually shows.
